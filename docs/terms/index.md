## What Verbal is

Verbal turns a spoken description of a job into a written, priced quote. You
describe the work, the app transcribes it on your device, and a language model
turns that text into a structured quote you can edit and send.

**The quote is your document, not ours.** You decide what goes on it, what it
says, and what you charge. Verbal helps you write it faster; it does not decide
your prices, check your figures, or advise you on the work.

## What the AI produces, and what it doesn't

The quote is generated by a language model from what you said. It can get
things wrong. It can mishear a number, miss an item, or misread an
instruction.

**Check every quote before you send it.** The prices, quantities and totals on a
quote you send are yours — you are the one making the offer to your customer,
and you are bound by it.

Verbal is deliberately built not to invent prices: an item with no price you
spoke and no match in your rate card is flagged rather than guessed. That is a
safeguard, not a guarantee. It does not make the rest of the quote correct, and
it does not remove your obligation to read what you are about to send.

Verbal is not accounting, tax, or legal advice. The tax rate applied to your
quotes is the one **you** set.
