## App Review notes

Verbal Pro removes only the daily limit on creating new quotes. It does not
unlock hidden content. Existing quotes remain readable, editable, shareable,
and deletable without a subscription. Purchases are processed by StoreKit;
Restore Purchases is available in both the paywall and Account screens.

## Privacy update for this release

The privacy policy now explains that Apple-signed subscription status, product,
expiry, and transaction identifiers are stored to provide Verbal Pro. Payment
card details, Apple ID, and billing address are never received by Verbal.
